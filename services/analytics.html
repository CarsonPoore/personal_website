When GA4 says zero and the phone won’t stop.

Owners tell us GA4 is broken. It rarely is. It’s misconfigured, almost always in one of four ways.

The form fill nobody flagged

GA4 records every form submission, then waits for a person to mark that event as a key event. Until someone does, your conversion report reads zero during a week when the phone never stopped. It’s the cause we find most. The fix takes minutes. Finding it is the work.

A tag firing twice, or not at all

Put Google Tag Manager in the wrong spot and it double-counts some pages while missing others. An old Universal Analytics snippet still sitting in your theme does its own damage. Before we trust any number you’ve already got, we trace where every tag fires.

The visitors no setup can see

Some of your visitors run ad blockers that stop analytics scripts cold. We can’t fix that. Neither can anyone else selling you a setup. What we can do is show you roughly where the gap sits. Call tracking closes some of it, since phones don’t run ad blockers.

Two tools, two different totals

GA4 and Google Ads each give credit for a sale by their own rules, and your CRM keeps a third set of books. So the totals never match, and it feels like somebody’s lying. Nobody is. We pick one rule, explain it to you once in plain English, and report by it every month after, so this month can be compared to last.

Every lead gets a return address.

Cleanup comes first: one property, one clean set of tags, referral spam filtered out, your own visits excluded. Then we wire up the four things that tie marketing to money.

Asked before anyone shares a screen.

Why is GA4 not tracking my conversions?

Usually one of four things: the event was never marked as a key event, a Google Tag Manager tag sits in the wrong spot, ad blockers hide some visitors, or two reports credit sales by different attribution rules. Check your key events first. It costs nothing, and it’s the culprit we find most.

How much does a GA4 consultant cost?

GA4 agencies sell a one-time setup, and some owners only need that. With us, ongoing tracking and the monthly report sit inside a retainer of $1,200, $2,400, or $4,800 a month, with a three-month minimum. A standalone cleanup gets a written quote before any work starts.

Do I need call tracking if I already have GA4?

If customers call you to buy, yes. GA4 only sees what happens inside the browser, so a call placed from your website looks like nothing happened. Call tracking shows a forwarding number to visitors from search and ads, and every call arrives tagged with where it came from. Your real number stays yours.

Can you set up GA4 for a business outside Indiana?

Yes. We’re based in Indianapolis and our local marketing work stays in central Indiana, but analytics happens over a screen share. Technical clients anywhere in the country get the same setup and monthly report at the same published prices.

Should I build my own Looker Studio dashboard?

You can, and some owners enjoy it. But a dashboard only redraws what GA4 collected. If the events underneath are wrong, the charts are wrong in nicer colors. Fix the tracking first, then decide whether you need a dashboard at all. One plain page usually does the job.
