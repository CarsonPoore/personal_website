The short version

What you’re buying, up front

The strategy engagement is one project with one price: $3,000, start to finish in about three weeks. We study your numbers and talk to your customers. Then we hand you a document that answers the question every owner eventually asks us: what should we do first?

You leave with four deliverables. Positioning. A defined best-fit customer. A sharp offer. And a prioritized 90-day plan, delivered in a live walkthrough, because a plan you don’t understand is a plan you won’t run.

We think strategy should be bought once and owned, not rented inside a retainer. So this stands alone. There’s no subscription attached and nothing that expires. For context, independent positioning and go-to-market sprints typically list between $1,500 and $3,000-plus on marketplaces like Contra and Maven. Ours sits at the top of that range, and it ends with a dated plan rather than a deck.

If you’re a founder, this is usually the front door. We usually point founders here first, before anyone touches a channel or spends a dollar on media.

What you leave with

Four deliverables and a walkthrough

The same four deliverables listed on the pricing page, spelled out. Everything arrives in plain English, written in your language, about your customers.

  1. 01

    Positioning you can say out loud.

    One clear answer to why somebody picks you over the shop down the road, written so anyone on your team says it the same way.

  2. 02

    Your people, defined.

    Who your best customers actually are, what they want, what stalls them, and what finally makes them buy.

  3. 03

    A sharp offer.

    Your core offer named, structured, and priced on purpose, including what to change and what to leave alone.

  4. 04

    The 90-day plan itself.

    Specific actions in a specific order, prioritized by payoff. We walk you through it live, so the reasoning transfers along with the list.

It stands alone on purpose. Hand it to your team or run it yourself. Everything in it belongs to you from the day we deliver it.

Fit check

Who this is for, and who should wait

A pattern worth naming first: founder-led selling stalls once the company only grows if you’re personally on the call. That usually happens well before $2 million in revenue, and it’s where this engagement earns its keep.

Start here if
  • You’re a founder somewhere between your first dollar and roughly $2M in revenue, and growth so far has mostly been you.
  • You’re an owner asking “what should we do first?” before spending another marketing dollar.
  • A past retainer produced activity instead of customers, and this time you want the thinking before any doing.
  • You’re considering one of our retainers and want the plan in hand first. Good order.
Wait if
  • You haven’t sold the thing consistently yet. Before product-market fit, a paid plan mostly says “go sell.” We’d rather send you off with homework.
  • You need leads this week. Strategy is a three-week project; an urgent push is a retainer conversation.
  • You already have a plan you believe in. Run it. Come back when it stops answering your questions.
  • $3,000 would strain the business right now. The free plan starter covers the first mile for nothing.

Yes, this section talks people out of paying us. That’s deliberate. An engagement sold to the wrong stage helps nobody, and a founder we send away early can come back when the timing is right. If you’re weighing stage fit, the founders page goes deeper.

After the walkthrough

The plan feeds everything else we do

The document doesn’t care who executes it. If the plan calls for campaigns, content, and email, that’s our Marketing practice. If it calls for a site or an ad account built right, that’s Technical. If you’d rather run it yourself with us checking your work each month, that’s Coaching & growth. And some owners simply run it alone, which still counts as the plan working.

The engagement and the retainers are priced separately on the pricing page. Buying one obligates you to nothing else.

Fair questions

What owners ask before buying the plan

What exactly do we get for the $3,000?

Four deliverables in one document: positioning, a defined best-fit customer, a sharp offer, and a prioritized 90-day plan. Then a live walkthrough, so the reasoning comes with it. It’s the same engagement described on our pricing page, and it’s yours to keep even if we never work together again.

How long does the strategy engagement take?

About three weeks from kickoff to walkthrough. Most of that is research and conversations with you and, where possible, a few of your customers. The writing comes last. If a week slips because your schedule is full, that’s normal and fine.

What if we just take the plan and run it ourselves?

That’s a win, and we mean it. The engagement is priced to stand alone, every deliverable is written so your team can execute without us, and running it yourself is a legitimate outcome. If you get stuck mid-plan, coaching exists for that. No obligation, no expiration.

Are we too early for this?

If you haven’t sold your product consistently yet, probably. Before product-market fit, the honest advice is to go sell, listen hard, and come back once real customers exist. So that’s what we’ll tell you on a free call, along with homework, instead of taking the $3,000.

What happens after the walkthrough?

Whatever you choose. Some owners run the plan themselves. Others hand the campaign work to our marketing practice, the builds to technical, or keep us on as a monthly advisor through coaching. The plan is written to work in any of those hands, including yours.
