GoHighLevel, set up so it sticks.

A GoHighLevel consultant takes the platform from blank account to the system your business runs on: pipelines, automations, email and SMS flows, and contact data you can trust. Ours lives inside a published monthly retainer, we work nationwide, and if the CRM you already have is doing its job, we'll tell you to keep it.

Book a free call → First: is GoHighLevel even worth it?

Everything starts blank.

The demo looks incredible. Then you sign up, log in, and there's nothing there. No pipelines, no sequences, no templates that match how you sell. GoHighLevel can replace your scheduler, your email platform, your forms, your review software, and your business texting, but on day one it replaces nothing. It just sits there, billing you.

That's the gap we close. Owners who abandon a CRM usually didn't pick bad software. They got to week three with leads still living in text threads and a legal pad, and went back to what they knew. The fix is unglamorous: someone has to wire the thing to your actual business, test it on real leads, and stay around while your team learns to trust it.

We think most CRM projects die in the import, not the demo. Wire the boring parts first.

What a setup engagement covers.

Four jobs. Each one gets finished, tested on live leads, and handed over documented, so the system keeps working on the day nobody's thinking about it.

From your old stack to one account

Account, domains, calendars, phone and A2P text registration, and the import. Coming from HubSpot, Keap, Jobber, or a spreadsheet? We export everything, map the fields, and spot-check against the old system before anything gets turned off. Nothing goes dark mid-switch. Your phones keep ringing the whole time.

Stages that match how you really sell

Not the default template. One pipeline per line of business, stages named in your words, so a ten-second glance on a Tuesday morning tells you exactly who needs a call today and which quotes have gone quiet. If a stage doesn't change what you do next, it doesn't exist.

Follow-up that happens without you

New-lead replies that go out in under a minute. Quote follow-ups that nudge twice and then stop. Win-back messages to customers you haven't seen in a year. We write them in your voice, you approve every word, and the sequences do the remembering so nobody has to.

The unsexy part that makes it all true

Duplicates merged, tags that mean one thing, dead contacts archived, text consent recorded properly. It's tedious and nobody puts it in a sales deck, but it decides whether your reports can be believed. Clean data here is also what makes our GA4 and analytics work worth reading later.

Three automations carry most of the weight.

You could build a hundred workflows in GoHighLevel. In a service business, three earn their keep first, so they go live in week one.

01

Missed-call text-back

You're mid-job, a call rolls to voicemail, and within seconds the caller gets a text: sorry we missed you, want a call back or a text? Callers who won't leave a voicemail will usually answer a text. One saved job covers the platform for the month.

02

Appointment reminders

A confirmation when the job is booked and a nudge the day before, by text. No-shows drop, reschedules happen before the slot is wasted, and nobody on your team spends Friday afternoon making reminder calls.

03

Review requests

The job closes, the customer gets one text with one link, at the moment they're happiest. A steady flow of recent Google reviews, with no begging and no awkward asks at the door. Set it once and it runs.

Built for people who answer from the truck.

GoHighLevel earns its keep fastest in the trades. When you're on a roof or under a sink, you physically cannot answer the phone, and the caller is already dialing your competitor. Missed-call text-back exists for exactly that moment. We set it up for Indianapolis-area contractors we can sit across a table from, and for home-services businesses anywhere in the country.

The numbers, all of them.

GoHighLevel's published plans run $97 to $497 a month. Metered usage for email, text, and AI features adds to that, and anyone quoting you only the plan price is leaving part of it out.

The case for it is consolidation. A service business paying separately for a scheduler, an email platform, forms, review software, and business texting often spends more on the pile than on the one platform that replaces it. At 5,000 contacts, GoHighLevel works out to about $3,564 a year; a comparable HubSpot setup is about $14,880, per both platforms' published pricing.

Our part: setup, migration, and ongoing automation live inside our monthly retainers of $1,200, $2,400, or $4,800, three months minimum, then month to month. No separate setup invoice. If you ever leave, the account, the contacts, and every workflow go with you.

Right for some. Wrong for plenty.

Worth it if
  • You run a service business and leads arrive by phone, text, and form, and some of them slip.
  • You're paying for five or more separate tools that don't talk to each other.
  • A team touches your customers, so follow-up can't live in one person's head.
  • You'll actually let the automations run instead of turning them off in week two.
Skip it if
  • You're solo with under about 50 leads a month. A shared inbox and a calendar app cover you, free.
  • Your current CRM works and the team uses it. Software churn is a tax, and we won't charge it.
  • You want a platform to fix a sales problem. It schedules follow-up; it can't close for you.

Already have a CRM your team opens every day? Keep it. We build around whatever you run. The AI and automation work doesn't care whose logo is on the login screen, and neither do we.

Days to functional. Weeks to humming.

The skeleton goes live fast: pipelines, calendars, missed-call text-back, and your booking links, usually inside five to seven days. The part nobody advertises is weeks two through eight, when we watch real leads move through the system and keep adjusting. A follow-up sequence tuned on live responses behaves differently than one written in a vacuum.

Fair warning: that middle stretch feels slow, and we'd rather tell you now than have you wonder in week four. By week eight it hums. That rhythm is also why the three-month minimum exists.

Asked before the first call.

What does a GoHighLevel consultant cost?

Independent GoHighLevel consultants quote setup one project at a time. We price it differently: setup, migration, and ongoing automation live inside our monthly retainers of $1,200, $2,400, or $4,800, three months minimum, then month to month. There is no separate setup invoice.

Is GoHighLevel worth it for a small business?

If you run a service business, leads reach you by phone and by form, and you're paying for five or more separate tools, usually yes. If you're a solo handling under about 50 leads a month, usually no; the subscription outruns the benefit. We wrote the full cost math, usage fees included, in our article “Is GoHighLevel worth it?” Read it before you buy anything.

How long does GoHighLevel setup take?

Five to seven days to functional: pipelines, calendars, missed-call text-back, and your booking links live. Six to eight weeks to humming, because the follow-up sequences need real leads flowing through them before they can be trusted. Anyone promising a complete build in a weekend is selling you a template.

Can you migrate us from HubSpot or another CRM?

Yes. We export the data, map the fields, dedupe, and spot-check against the old system before anything gets switched off. And if that review shows your current CRM is doing its job, we'll tell you to keep it and build the automation around it instead. Switching has a real cost in attention; we don't recommend it casually.

Do you work outside Indianapolis?

Yes. CRM and automation work is fully remote, and we run it for clients nationwide. Indianapolis is home, so local trades and home-services businesses also get us in person. Either way, the same team that writes your plan builds your account, and we take eight clients at a time so that stays true.
