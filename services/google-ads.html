Google Ads, run like it’s your money.

Honest Google Ads management for Indianapolis service businesses. Search ads that catch people the night the furnace quits, sent to a landing page built to convert them, with tracking wired in before the first dollar spends. You set the budget. You own the account. Every month, a report that counts calls and booked work instead of impressions.

What you’re paying for, week to week.

“Management” can hide a lot of nothing, so here’s ours, itemized. Four jobs, each one visible in the monthly report.

Ads built from your actual jobs

We start with two questions the last company probably skipped: what an average job is worth to you, and which work you want more of. Then the campaigns get built around how people here really search. “Water heater replacement Greenwood.” “Emergency electrician near me.” Just as important is the negative list, the searches we refuse to pay for, starting with job hunters and DIYers.

A page built to catch the click

Sending paid traffic to your homepage is a quick way to kill an ad budget. Each ad points to a page that matches it: same service, same town, a phone number up top, a short form for after-hours, loading fast on a phone in a parking lot. If your current site can’t host that, we build the page as part of setup.

Tracking wired in before launch

We won’t spend your money blind. Call tracking and conversion tracking go in before the first ad runs, with GA4 configured so a booked job in week nine traces back to the search that started it. This is the unglamorous plumbing of the whole system. Nobody sees it, so it gets skipped.

Budgets you set, tuning that doesn’t stop

You decide the monthly spend, and it never moves without your yes. Our job is making each dollar less wasteful than the last: pruning search terms that click but never call, and moving budget toward the towns and jobs that book. Week after week. Quietly.

Stop paying rent on your own customers.

If you run a trade around Indianapolis, you’ve probably tried Angi or HomeAdvisor. The leads arrive cold, they’re shared with whoever else pays, and the moment you stop paying, the flow stops with nothing left behind. Home-services comparisons from Housecall Pro and Hearth put platform leads at roughly $80 each, against about $45 from a well-run Google Ads account you own. The price gap is only half the argument. The other half is what accumulates: your own ads teach you which searches become booked jobs, and that history stays in your account even after we’re gone. It’s the thinking behind most of the work we do for local businesses.

Sometimes the answer is don’t run ads yet.

Here’s our opinion, and it costs us money every time we share it: most local businesses buy Google Ads too early. Ads amplify what’s already there. If these three things aren’t true yet, we’ll tell you so on the free call and point you at the cheaper fix first.

  1. Your offer gives people a reason to pick you

    A stranger comparing three tabs should be able to see why you over the other two: a real price, a same-week slot, a guarantee, anything concrete. Until that answer exists, ads mostly fund the comparison shopping. Sharpening the offer is strategy work, and it comes first.

  2. Someone catches the lead in minutes

    Ads make the phone ring within days. If calls roll to voicemail at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday and form fills wait until Friday, you’d be paying premium prices for leads nobody answers. Fix the follow-up before you buy the traffic. We build that too.

  3. Your Google profile can close the deal

    People who click an ad check your reviews before they call. A thin profile leaks paid clicks all day. If that’s where you are, start with local SEO and come back; it’s the cheaper win, and it makes every ad dollar later work harder.

If that’s you, we’ll send you away with that homework and no invoice. It stings a little. But we cap ourselves at eight clients at a time, and those eight hired us for results they can count, so the gate stays.

A report you can read in the truck.

When vcita surveyed 500 US small-business owners in 2025, 60% of those who’d left a marketing agency said they couldn’t see the return. We’ve read the reports that cause that. Impressions, reach, click-through rates, “the algorithm.” Ours is one page: how many people called, how many became booked work, what you spent on ads and on us, and the one thing we’re changing next month. If a number went the wrong way, it’s in there, with the reason.

Asked by owners, answered straight.

What does Google Ads management cost in Indianapolis?

Management runs inside our published retainers: $1,200, $2,400, or $4,800 a month, depending on how much of your marketing we handle alongside the ads. Ad spend is separate. It goes to Google directly, from an ad account you own, and you set the number. Three months minimum, then month to month. The full rate card is on our pricing page.

How much should I spend on the ads themselves?

There’s no universal number, and anyone who quotes one without asking what an average job is worth to you is guessing. We set the budget together on the first call, based on your job value and how far you’ll drive. We’d rather you hold a modest budget steady for three months than panic-pause a big one in week two. Our free Budget Calculator gives you an honest starting range.

How fast will Google Ads bring in leads?

Clicks start within days of launch. Useful performance takes longer, because the first weeks are spent finding out which searches turn into booked jobs and cutting the ones that don’t. That tuning is most of what management is, and it’s why we ask for three months before you judge the results. You’ll see the movement in the report every month either way.

Another company runs our ads now. Can you tell us if it’s working?

Yes, and this is one of our favorite calls. Bring your account login to a free call and we’ll read the last 90 days together: what the money bought and whether anything is tracked well enough to judge. If your current company is doing good work, we’ll say that too. It happens.

Who owns the ad account?

You do, from day one. The account is created in your name, the card on file is yours, and every search term and result stays with you. If you ever leave us, you keep the account, the history, the landing pages, and the tracking. Nothing needs transferring, because none of it ever left your hands.

Wondering what the money’s actually buying?

Book a free call and bring your ad account, or just your doubts. We’ll read the last 90 days out loud in plain English, and you’ll leave knowing what’s worth keeping and what to fix first. No charge either way.
