Marketing practice

Marketing measured in booked work, not impressions.

Brand, campaigns, content, and email for Indianapolis small businesses, from a team that counts calls and customers and will tell you what not to buy.

Book a free call → See what it costs

The work is still good. The phone got quiet.

Word of mouth built your business. Years of solid jobs, neighbors telling neighbors. Then it stopped scaling. Now the company down the road shows up first on Google and you don’t show up at all.

Maybe you’ve paid for marketing before. A thousand a month, a report full of charts and buzzwords, and no more jobs than the month before. That wound is common enough to measure: in vcita’s April 2025 survey of 500 small-business owners, 40% had dropped or switched their marketing agency, and six in ten of those left because they couldn’t see what the money bought.

So here is how we run things, plainly. Prices are published on this site. Three months minimum, then month to month. Eight clients at a time. If you ever leave, you keep everything: the website, the Google Business Profile, the ad accounts. They’re registered to you from day one.

And the promise is concrete. When Indianapolis searches for what you do, you show up. When someone calls, they get an answer. If that first part isn’t happening today, start with why your business isn’t showing up on Google.

One opinion up front: most local businesses get sold social media management far too early. If your Google Business Profile is thin, fix that first. It’s free. And it’s where your customers actually look.

Half of this job is telling you where not to spend.

Four kinds of work, one practice

Everything below is done for you and reported every month, aimed at one outcome: more of the right customers finding you and coming back.

Brand & messaging

The look and the words that make you the obvious choice. Before we touch a logo, we ask about your average job value and your best customers. The last company never asked. We always do.

Campaigns

Seasonal pushes with a budget and a goal counted in calls. Fix the pipeline before the spring rush instead of panicking in the middle of it, and fill the slow Friday nights on purpose.

Content

Your actual work, photographed and written up so Google and your neighbors can find it. Content is slow. We say that up front, because it’s also the thing that compounds.

Email

The unglamorous one. A steady note to past customers beats almost everything else on this page, because those people already trust you. It costs nearly nothing to send.

Brand identity, photography, and video production are available as project work, quoted on their own on the pricing page. Retainer clients can add a project anytime.

Where marketing ends, the other practices pick up

Honestly, the four practices blur. A campaign usually needs a landing page, and a new lead needs an answer inside five minutes; that build work is our Technical practice. The owner asking “what should we do first?” wants the one-time Strategy engagement before anyone spends a dollar on this page. And if you’d rather run marketing yourself with us advising, that’s Coaching & growth. Same team, one hallway. On the first call we’ll tell you which door you’re actually standing in front of.

Questions owners actually ask

What does marketing cost for a small business in Indianapolis?

Our retainers are $1,200, $2,400, or $4,800 a month, published on the pricing page with what each month includes. For context, SBA guidance puts marketing at 7–8% of gross revenue for businesses under $5 million, so $1,200 a month is a normal commitment for a business doing around $200,000 a year.

Do you lock clients into long contracts?

No. The minimum is three months, because real change takes that long. After that it’s month to month. Everything we build is registered to you from day one, so if you leave, it all stays yours.

How will we know if it’s working?

You’ll get a monthly report in plain English that counts calls and booked work, not impressions. Call tracking goes in during the first week, so you can see which jobs came from where.

Do you work with businesses that compete with us?

No. We cap the whole consultancy at eight clients at a time, and we won’t take two of the same trade in the same territory. If your competitor got here first, we’ll tell you and point you somewhere good.

What if we’re not ready for ads?

Then we’ll say so and not sell them to you. Most local businesses we meet should fix their Google Business Profile and reviews before spending a dollar on ads. If that’s you, we’ll start there, or just tell you how to do it yourself.

Tell us what a good month looks like.

A free 30-minute call. We’ll ask about your service area and your average job, the questions the last company never asked. If we’re not the right buy, we’ll say so.
