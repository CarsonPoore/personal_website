Five steps, in an order that actually matters.

Most marketing starts in the middle, with the ads and the posts, then wonders why nothing sticks. We start at the beginning, with you, and build forward one step at a time.

01Conviction 02Your people 03A sharp offer 04The experience 05Word of mouth
01

Conviction

We start with you. What you believe about your work, why you built it, what you won’t compromise on. It sounds soft. It isn’t. Your conviction is the one thing no competitor can copy, and everything else stands on it.

Conviction is the opening of our strategy engagement, the one-time $3,000 project that begins with what you stand for and ends with a 90-day plan.

02

Your people

Next, we get specific about who your marketing is for. Not “everyone.” The customers who light up when they find you. Marketing to the right people is calm and effective. Marketing to everyone is expensive and exhausting.

Who “your people” are depends on who you are, so we wrote it out three ways, for local businesses, founders, and nonprofits.

03

A sharp offer

Then we make what you sell impossible to misunderstand. Named, packaged, priced, and pointed. A sharp offer does the selling for you, so you spend less time convincing and more time doing what you’re good at.

Shaping your offer is the middle of the strategy engagement, and our own prices sit in the open on the pricing page as a working example.

04

The experience

Every touchpoint, from first impression to tenth visit. Here’s what most marketing misses: being your customer is your marketing. We design those moments on purpose, so the business starts selling itself.

This is where the building happens: websites and web apps for the first impression, and GoHighLevel and CRM for what happens after someone raises a hand.

05

Word of mouth, on purpose

Last, we build the part most businesses leave to chance: people talking about you. Delight is the only marketing that compounds for free. Get the first four steps right and this one starts taking care of itself.

A recommendation usually ends with “just look them up,” so this step shows up as local SEO work: your Google profile, your reviews, and the answer to “who should I call?”
