Things owners ask first.

What does a marketing consultant cost in Indianapolis?

Our retainers are $1,200, $2,400, or $4,800 a month, and the one-time strategy engagement is $3,000. Nonprofits pay a reduced rate. The pricing page lists what each tier includes, so you can compare us without booking anything.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Engagements run three months minimum, then month to month. We ask for three because real change takes that long. After that, leave whenever you like. You keep your site, your Google Business Profile, your ad accounts, and your data.

Who actually does the work?

We do. The same small team that writes your plan builds the site, the automation, the ad account, and the reporting. Nothing is resold to subcontractors, and the roster stops at eight clients so it stays that way.

Do you only work with Indianapolis businesses?

Marketing and local SEO stay close to home, within about an hour of Indianapolis, because local work should be done by people who are actually local. Technical and coaching engagements run nationwide over video.
