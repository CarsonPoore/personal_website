The problem

Most small business marketing is just a pile of random tactics.

A boosted post one week. A new logo the next. An ad that ran for a month, then quietly stopped. None of it talks to the rest, so none of it adds up. You stay busy, nothing much changes, and it’s easy to think you’re the problem. You’re not. The pieces were never built to work together.
