— 02 · About

Not the traditional kind of marketer.

CP · ’26

Entrepreneur, marketing consultant, and Christian creative in Greater Indianapolis — building marketing products that actually help small businesses. I chose a college-alternative route through Vertical Immersive because the fastest way to get where I'm going was to actually start going.

I think differently by design. I'm drawn to bold ideas, contrarian strategy, and creative thinking that makes people question why no one tried it sooner. I don't move slow. I don't wait for permission. And I don't believe in safe marketing — because safe marketing doesn't change anything.

My faith shapes how I lead. Real influence is built on integrity. People matter more than metrics. The best leaders make the people around them better. That's the kind of marketer — and person — I'm building myself to be.

What I'm chasing isn't just a career. It's a seat at the table where bold decisions get made. Where creative strategy shapes culture. Where the work actually means something.

06 / On branding & AI 2 MO · FEATURED

When everyone looks professional, professional stops standing out.

AI can generate a logo in ten seconds. It can write captions, build websites, invent "brand guides" instantly. So branding in 2026 isn't design — it's identity. It's what people assume about you before they ever meet you. AI can generate content. It can't generate conviction. It can't generate a story. It can't generate presence. In a world where everything is automated, the brands that win will be the ones that still feel human.
