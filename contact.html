Before the call

A few things to sit with before we talk.

You don’t need answers ready. These just make the conversation better.

  1. Are you trying to launch something new, or improve what’s already running?
  2. Who is the customer you’d most like more of?
  3. What have you tried so far, and how did it feel?
  4. Are you open to being a little visible in your own marketing?
  5. A year from now, what does success actually look like for you?
