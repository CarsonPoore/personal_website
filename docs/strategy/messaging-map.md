## Master narrative
Carson Poore Consulting is a small Indianapolis team that plans the work and then builds it. The site used to say "marketing agency"; the truth is simpler and bigger: a growth consultancy with four practices — marketing, technical, strategy, and coaching — for local businesses, founders, and nonprofits across central Indiana, and anywhere for the technical and coaching work. The model is the message: prices published on the site ($1,200, $2,400, or $4,800 a month, a $3,000 one-time strategy engagement, a reduced nonprofit rate), eight clients total, three months minimum then month to month, and you keep everything — your site, your Google profile, your ad accounts — if you ever leave. Indy agencies have moved upmarket, AI shops can't do brand, and most fractional CMOs hand you a plan without hands. We sit in the intersection none of them occupy, run every engagement through five plain-English steps (Conviction, Your people, A sharp offer, The experience, Word of mouth), and talk like the candid friend who happens to be a strategist: we'll tell you what not to buy, when you're too early, and exactly what a month of work gets you.

## Practice one-liners
- **Marketing:** Brand, campaigns, content, and email that make the right people notice you and talk about you — measured in calls, customers, and booked work, not impressions.
- **Technical:** The builds most marketing shops can't do — AI agents and automation, websites and web apps, GoHighLevel and CRM, Google Ads, GA4 — practical, human-approved, and priced for small businesses.
- **Strategy:** A $3,000, one-time engagement: your positioning, your people, a sharp offer, and a 90-day plan you could run without us. The answer to "what should we do first?"
- **Coaching & growth:** A fractional CMO for owners and founders at $1,200–$4,800 a month — under the $3,000–$15,000 that 2026 pricing guides (MarketerHire, GoFractional) report for the category, with the plan and the hands on one team.

## ICP messages
- **Local small businesses:** Word of mouth built your business; it just stopped scaling. When Indianapolis searches for what you do, you'll show up — and every call, text, and form gets answered in minutes. Published prices, three months then month to month, monthly reports that count calls and booked jobs, and you keep everything if you ever leave. We'll also tell you what not to buy — starting with ads you're not ready for.
- **Founders & startups:** You built the thing; now we get it to people. Start with the $3,000 strategy engagement — positioning, who it's actually for, a first repeatable channel — then keep us on as your fractional CMO at well under category rates. We think like owners, own outcomes instead of deliverables, and build the automation too. If you're pre-product-market fit, we'll say so and send you off with homework instead of a retainer.
- **Nonprofits:** You're wearing five hats and marketing falls to whoever has ten spare minutes. Start with a free Ad Grant checkup — Google offers verified nonprofits $10,000 a month in free ads, and most orgs use a few hundred dollars of it — then one donor list everyone trusts, stories people understand, and numbers your board can read. The nonprofit rate isn't charity: the budgets are smaller and the work matters.

## Differentiators (each pre-answers a researched objection)
1. Published pricing on the site — most firms make you book a call to hear a number.
2. Eight clients, total. A team that knows your business, not a pod juggling forty accounts.
3. One team, four practices: the people who write the plan also build the automation, the site, and the ad account. Indy agencies don't build, AI shops don't do brand, fractional CMOs don't implement.
4. Actually in Indianapolis: real neighborhoods, real local clients, one exact NAP everywhere.
5. Five plain-English steps, deliberately untrademarked — no Growth Gears®, no framework soup.
6. Candor as policy: what not to buy, when you're too early, and "three months minimum, because real change takes that long" instead of fake no-contract promises.
7. You keep everything if you leave — site, Google Business Profile, ad accounts, data.
8. Five ungated, instantly-scored free tools (no email, no login) that double as working proof of the technical practice.
