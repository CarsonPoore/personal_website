# Research: aeo-geo

## Summary
AEO/GEO in late 2026 rewards sites that answer questions directly, prove claims with sourced statistics, keep content visibly fresh, and present one unambiguous entity across the web. The strongest evidence: the Princeton GEO research line showed adding statistics (+41%) and citations (+40%) lifts generative-engine visibility; Ahrefs data shows AI-cited pages are meaningfully fresher than organic results; and ChatGPT citations still correlate heavily with Bing rankings, making Bing Webmaster Tools indexing non-optional. Schema evidence is mixed — Ahrefs found no direct citation correlation, but structured data remains the cheapest way to feed entity resolution (Organization, sameAs, NAP) — and llms.txt is cheap to ship but mostly unread by major assistant crawlers (Ahrefs: 97% of files got zero AI requests in May 2026), so treat it as low-cost insurance, not a lever. For "best marketing consultant in Indianapolis"-type queries, third-party corroboration (reviews, directories, Google Business Profile, Reddit/forum mentions) matters more than anything on carsonpoore.com itself — brands are cited via third-party sources roughly 6.5x more than via their own domains.

## Key findings
- Princeton/Georgia Tech/IIT Delhi/AI2 GEO paper (KDD 2024) remains the canonical research: adding statistics lifted generative-engine visibility ~41%, adding citations/quotes ~40%; keyword stuffing had little or negative effect. Follow-up industry replications through 2025-2026 consistently confirm quotation, statistics, and source-citation as the top content-level levers.
- Ahrefs 2026 study of ~16.975M AI-cited URLs: cited sources are ~26% fresher on average than traditional organic results (25.7% gap by publish date, 13.1% by last-updated date). Visible dateModified and genuine content updates are a real citation factor.
- Ahrefs' large-scale study found NO meaningful correlation between schema markup presence and being cited by AI. Vendor claims like '2.7x more citations with JSON-LD' or 'FAQPage cited 340% more' circulate widely but are unverified vendor numbers — do not rely on them. Schema's real value is entity disambiguation (Organization + sameAs + NAP), which feeds the knowledge-graph lookup layer AI systems use before retrieval.
- ChatGPT Search runs on Bing's index for live retrieval; studies found ChatGPT citations matched Bing top-10 results ~87% of the time (vs ~56% for Google). If a page is not indexed in Bing, ChatGPT cannot cite it. One mid-2025 data point showed the Bing alignment weakening, so cover both: Bing Webmaster Tools AND Google Search Console.
- ChatGPT cites only ~15% of pages it retrieves; selection favors pages with question-shaped headings, an immediate direct answer, freshness signals, and clear authority attribution.
- The strongest on-page pattern among AI-cited sources: a question-phrased H2/H3, an immediate 40-70 word direct answer, then supporting depth. Content buried under preamble does not get extracted.
- llms.txt adoption is real but consumption is not: adoption grew ~8.8x (June 2025 to May 2026, ~36k sites; ~5.9-10% of crawled domains), yet Ahrefs found 97% of llms.txt files received ZERO AI-crawler requests in May 2026; of the rest, GPTBot made 4.51% of requests, ClaudeBot 0.80%. No major LLM vendor has confirmed fetching it at answer time. Ship it (10 minutes, Markdown at root, H1 + blockquote + H2 link sections), but expect nothing from it alone. Google did add llms.txt awareness to Lighthouse tooling, suggesting the standard may still mature.
- Brands are ~6.5x more likely to be cited by AI via third-party sources (Wikipedia, Reddit, directories, review platforms, industry publications) than via their own domain. For 'best marketing consultant in Indianapolis' queries, off-site presence IS the ranking factor.
- SOCi 2026 Local Visibility Index: ChatGPT recommends only ~1.2% of local businesses, and fewer than half of brands strong in traditional local search also appear in AI recommendations. ChatGPT sources local data from Google Business Profile, Bing Places, and review aggregates (Yelp), favoring complete profiles with positive sentiment. GBP completeness + Bing Places + review velocity are the local AI stack.
- Entity inconsistency (name/address/phone variants across site, GBP, LinkedIn, directories) appeared in 100% of professional-service firms in one 50-audit analysis — it is the most universal AI-visibility gap. Exact-match NAP everywhere plus Organization.sameAs arrays pointing at every official profile resolves it.
- Fast pages matter: one 2026 industry analysis found fast-loading pages ~3x more likely to be cited by ChatGPT; AI retrieval crawlers abandon slow pages. Static HTML is inherently advantaged here — server-rendered visible text, since most AI crawlers execute little or no JavaScript.
- 93% of AI search sessions end without a click (industry estimate), so the goal shifts from traffic to being the named recommendation; measurement shifts to prompt-level citation testing (asking ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude the money questions monthly and logging who gets named).
- Pricing transparency is a direct AEO weapon: 'what does a fractional CMO cost' queries get answered from pages that publish actual numbers. Carson's published pricing ($1,200/$2,400/$4,800 retainers, $3,000 strategy engagement) is exactly the kind of concrete, citable data LLMs prefer over 'contact us for pricing' competitors.
- Freshness signals that work: visible 'Last updated [date]' on page, dateModified in Article schema, genuine substantive updates (not date-bumping), and current-year references in titles/content where honest.
- Author attribution matters: bylined content with a Person entity (bio page, sameAs to LinkedIn, credentials) is favored by answer engines applying E-E-A-T-like filters; anonymous content is at a disadvantage for YMYL-adjacent topics like pricing and business advice.
- Reviews now account for ~20% of local pack ranking weight (up ~4 points since 2023), and active review profiles on AI-cited platforms correlated with ~3x higher ChatGPT citation likelihood in one Jan 2026 analysis (ConvertMate — single-vendor, treat as directional).
- Perplexity leans heavily on recently-crawled, well-structured pages and rewards pages that read like self-contained answers with their own citations; Google AI Overviews (~25% of searches) draw mostly from pages already ranking organically, so traditional SEO remains the entry ticket for Google's AI surfaces.
- Conversational/semantic coverage beats keyword matching: AI engines retrieve passages, not pages. Each page should cover a question cluster in natural language (the way a client would ask it aloud), with each passage able to stand alone when extracted.
