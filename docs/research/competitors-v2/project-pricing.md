# Project-pricing benchmark (CPC starting prices vs market)

Researched Oct 9, 2026. Every figure below is a vendor-published estimate or a provider's own price page; none is an independent survey. Local (Indianapolis/Midwest) data is thin: very few Indy providers publish numbers. "National" is labeled where used. Where a source did not state a number, it says "not published".

## Source quality notes
- Indianapolis web figures come mainly from one local marketing firm's promotional page (marketingbymagnet.com, which pitches its own AI site builder) and one named Fishers, IN studio (Sharp Guys). Treat the freelancer/agency bands as ballpark.
- Nearly all national guides are written by sellers (agencies, logo/video shops, cost-calculator vendors).
- No Indianapolis video or brand-photography company publishes a package price I could verify. Beverly Boy's Indianapolis pages are SEO-style and their rate sheet is internally inconsistent (garbled upper bounds), so I did not use their numbers as benchmarks.

## Evidence by category

### 1. Small-business websites
- Indianapolis, freelancer: $1,500-$4,500; local agency: $3,000-$10,000 for a 3-10 page site; $60-$125/hr for changes; agencies often add $50-$150/mo maintenance. Typically 2-3 revision rounds included; copywriting often excluded; booking/e-commerce adds four figures. https://marketingbymagnet.com/web-designer-cost-indianapolis (promotional source, undated "2026")
- Fishers, IN (Indy metro) Sharp Guys Web Design: custom websites start at $7,500, fixed fee, $230/mo maintenance plan. https://sharpguyswebdesign.com/faq-items/website-pricing/ . A search summary also attributes a 10% nonprofit discount and "$25,000 or more" for larger sites to the same firm; I did not confirm that on the fetched page.
- National freelancer: $500-$5,000 typical, $5,000-$10,000 premium; agency $5,000-$15,000 typical small-business site, $15,000-$60,000+ feature-rich; freelancer maintenance $50-$150/mo. https://www.levitate.ai/blog-posts/average-website-design-cost-for-small-businesses-in-2026 (no survey cited)
- National WordPress agency (Freshy): 5-8 page small-business site $500-$4,000 on a standard theme, plain plugins, contact forms, responsive. Hosting, domain, premium plugins and maintenance excluded. https://freshysites.com/blog/web-design-cost/
- Chicago: focused small-business site roughly $3,000-$8,000 (freelancer/solo); boutique agency $15,000-$40,000. https://cliquestudios.com/cost-of-web-design-in-chicago/ (Clique's page itself shows $5,000-$15,000 for a 5-10 page business site fixed fee.)
- Marketplace floor (national): Wix Marketplace pros list "starting at" $100-$500 for a basic Wix site, $300-$1,500 for classic sites. https://www.wix.com/marketplace/hire/web-design/classic-website
- DIY: Wix/Squarespace roughly $192-$552/yr. https://marketingbymagnet.com/web-designer-cost-indianapolis

### 2. Custom web app
- Freelancer MVP $10,000-$40,000; agency MVP $25,000-$80,000; simple app (login, basic data ops, dashboard) $10,000-$25,000 freelancer, $25,000-$50,000 agency; US dev rate $101-$225/hr. https://projectcostestimator.com/blog/web-app-development-cost-2026 (vendor of a cost calculator)
- A second estimator in the same search set gives a lower freelancer MVP band, $3,000-$15,000 (median $9,000), and agency $8,000-$40,000. https://projectcostestimator.com/mvp-cost-calculator (seen in search result only, not fetched)
- No-code MVP platform fees $500-$2,000 (same search result set).

### 3. Logo and brand identity
- Freelance logo $300-$2,000 for most small businesses; low $300; contests $249-$1,000+; design subscription $699-$1,199/mo; branding agency $3,000-$10,000+ for identity system and guidelines (page says prices checked Sept 2026). https://www.manypixels.co/blog/brand-design/logo-design-cost-guide
- Freelance logo $300-$800 professional; agency logo $800-$2,500+; contests $299-$1,299; full identity packages $3,000-$20,000; budget $500-$1,500 for a logo that works in print and digital. https://freshysites.com/?p=59459
- Brand identity by tier (source dated June 19, 2026, internally inconsistent): entry freelancer $300-$1,500 (logo + palette); experienced freelancer $2,000-$10,000 (logo suite, color, type, guidelines); boutique agency $10,000-$30,000. https://www.manypixels.co/blog/brand-design/brand-identity-design-services
- Indiana state-level estimate (generic guide, seller-oriented): logo $225-$4,500; full branding $450-$9,000; $36-$113/hr. https://startcosts.com/graphic-design/indiana
- UK report (pounds, so only directional): starter pack logo + guidelines GBP 1,500-5,000, described as right for most small businesses. https://whito.co.uk/research/branding-design-costs-uk/

### 4. Small-business brand video
- Per type: simple social clip/talking head $500-$2,500; testimonial $800-$3,000; brand/about-us video $2,500-$8,000; explainer with motion graphics $3,000-$10,000. Freelancers at the low end of each band. No city-specific data. https://alwaysdobetterllc.com/how-much-does-video-production-cost/
- Another guide: most business video $1,000-$10,000; brand videos from $5,000, with most companies spending $12,000-$30,000 (production-company bias). https://c-istudios.com/?p=83854
- Indianapolis: Clark Entertainment Group listed by a directory with a $5,000+ minimum project (secondhand, via Gartner profile in search results). Drone add-on $1,000-$1,500 on Beverly Boy's Indy page. No verified Indy brand-video package price.

### 5. Short-form video packages
- $99/mo for 4 videos up to $629/mo for 30 videos, 15-60 sec, branded, captions, optional posting, month-to-month, 3 revision rounds in month 1 (appears to be template/AI-assisted, no on-camera shoot). https://www.99dollarsocial.com/short-form-video
- Editing-only freelance retainer: 12+ videos from $1,500/mo (~$125/video; client supplies footage). https://contra.com/s/qsLFcr6L-monthly-short-form-video-editing-12-videos
- Full-service: $2,500/mo for 10 videos with 6-month commitment (~$250/video); another provider $3,000-$8,000/mo for 8-25 videos. Both found via search snippets on small vendor pages (not fetched), not Midwest-specific.

### 6. Half-day photography
- National: half-day (about 4 hrs) $500-$1,500; full day $1,500-$4,000+; hourly $75-$250. Midwest (e.g., Columbus, Kansas City): half-day $400-$900, full day $900-$2,200. 30-150 edited images typical; entry-level sessions 20-40. Web/social usage usually included; print/broadcast adds 20-50%. Heavy retouching $25-$100/image and travel/props billed separately. Photographers with 1-2 years' experience often $200-$500 per half day. https://www.biztoolkit.co/post/brand-photographer-rates-in-2026-per-session-package-pricing
- Indianapolis listings (short sessions only): branding portraits $250 for 30 min (https://www.airbnb.com/services/6076053); 1-hour package that includes branding $350 (https://www.airbnb.com/services/6103578); headshots $150/30 min (https://www.airbnb.com/services/6081070).
- Snappr: US marketplace rate $89 first hour (value) or $169 first hour (all-inclusive), market range $139-$300/hr; rates set per country not city. https://snappr.com/how-much-does-a-photographer-cost/product/indianapolis-in
- Indianapolis product shoot average about $627 for four hours per a search summary of Snappr (2020 data), not confirmed on fetched page.

## Risk of underpricing (what clients assume at the number)
- $900 website: assumes a custom design, unlimited revisions, copywriting included, hosting and SEO covered. Also anchors every later upsell and attracts price-shoppers comparing to Wix pros at $100-$500. Cheap quotes commonly assume the client writes the text (marketingbymagnet), and that is exactly the scope that blows up margin.
- $2,500 business site: low risk. Still needs a page cap, because Indy agencies quote $3,000-$10,000 for the same 3-10 pages.
- $6,000 web app: clients assume a working SaaS with login, payments and admin. Estimators price auth alone at $1,500-$3,000 and role-based access at $5,000-$12,000. Either you lose money or the page looks unserious to anyone who has priced an app.
- $750 logo: little risk, but unlimited concept rounds are the trap. Source guides note scope creep (favicon, app icon, social crops) can double a single-mark quote.
- $2,000 brand system: clients assume strategy, messaging, guidelines, stationery and templates. Market treats $2,000 as the start of "experienced freelancer" pricing; agency identity starts at $3,000.
- $1,200 brand video: assumes a polished multi-scene piece with script, crew and licensed music. Market brand video is $2,500-$8,000.
- $750 short-form pack: assumes a monthly volume (10+) and filming. At $125-$250 per video market per-video rates, $750 buys 3-6 videos.
- $350 half-day shoot: assumes 4 hours, 40-80 retouched images, and usage rights. Market half day is $400-$900 in the Midwest.

## Presentation advice
- Show "from" prices with the scope line visible, not hidden on a quote. The scope is what protects margin and signals quality.
- Bundle where it helps: half-day shoot + short-form pack ($500 + $750) is a credible content day and makes the shoot price look like part of a package rather than a standalone cheap rate.
- Nonprofit reduced rate (already offered) is common; Sharp Guys offers a nonprofit discount (secondhand). Keep it as an explicit percentage off these figures rather than a lower base list price.
- Because retainers start at $1,200/mo, a $750 pack or $900 site sitting next to them is consistent as an entry offer; a $350 shoot or $1,200 "brand video" looks inconsistent with a $1,200 retainer that includes more.
