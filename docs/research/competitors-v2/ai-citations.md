Single run per prompt; AI answers vary run to run, so treat as a snapshot, not a rank. Only Perplexity was tested (no ChatGPT/Gemini/Google AI Overviews). carsonpoore.com was rebuilt Oct 7-8; Perplexity had not re-crawled it.

## Analysis

### What kinds of pages get cited
Counts across the 8 prompts (approx. 80 citations):
- Third-party directories and ranked lists (Clutch, Sortlist, BBB, Thumbtack, Semrush agency directory, The Manifest, Expertise.com, UpCity, DesignRush, seobackoffice, SiteCity): dominant for the 3 local "who/best" prompts; nearly every local answer's citation list is directories.
- Vendor own-site pages: dominant for informational and cost prompts (fractional CMO cost, GHL, AI agents, budget). The cited firms are small agencies or consultants writing one specific-question article. Local fractional CMO prompt also cites vendors' own "[service] in Indianapolis" location pages (MarkCMO, Josh Ramsey, ModFX, Marketing Guardians, Lifted Logic, Carissa Newton).
- Authority/official: Google support pages for Ad Grants, SBA and QuickBooks/Mercury/banks for budget. These set the facts; vendors supply the local recommendation.
- LinkedIn: individual profiles (Rich Guth, Rob Higley) for fractional CMO; a LinkedIn Pulse article for AI agents; LinkedIn services directory. Moderate presence.
- Reddit: not cited in any of the 8 answers. It appears only in search results for "Indianapolis marketing consultant" (r/indianapolis, r/Entrepreneur), low weight.
- Press release wire (openpr.com) was the only source for the one local Ad Grants provider.

### Format of cited passages
- Question-as-heading with a one- or two-sentence direct answer containing a number ("typically range from $8,000 to $20,000 per month for 20 to 40 hours") - MarkCMO, ModFX, Lifted Logic, Carissa Newton all use FAQ blocks of this shape.
- Tables with name, specialty, best for, revenue range, rate, location (strategymavenagency).
- Ranges with tiers and drivers; explicit year in title ("2026 hiring guide", "blog-fractional-cmo-cost-2026").
- Numbers in the first line. Perplexity's own answers lead with bolded figures and verdicts ("Sometimes", "5-10% of revenue").
- Directory entries: name, rating, review count, price band, team size, location (Clutch/Manifest). Price badge fields appear ("$1,000+, $300+/hr" for the one Clutch fractional CMO).

### What CPC pages should change to become citable
Already in place (verified by curl on Oct 9): home, /about, /investment, services, articles, tools return 200 to Mozilla, GPTBot, PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot UAs; robots.txt explicitly allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Bingbot; sitemap lists 38 URLs, lastmod 2026-10-08; llms.txt (6.2 KB, practices/services/articles/tools with prices); JSON-LD on home (ProfessionalService, Person, WebSite, FAQPage), articles (Article, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage with 5 Q&As, dateModified), service pages (Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList), /investment (OfferCatalog, Offer, PriceSpecification), /about (AboutPage, Person).

### Other things AI crawlers would want
- A /pricing or /investment copy of prices in plain HTML (present) and in llms.txt (present); also consider a plain-text FAQ page, as FAQ blocks are the cited unit.
- Consistent NAP/GBP data and a Google Business Profile with category "Marketing consultant"; local answers pull from directories keyed on NAP.
- Dated, versioned changes (dateModified is present on articles).
- Canonical tags present on home; confirm all pages carry canonicals and og tags (checked home only).

## Gaps
- Only Perplexity was tested; ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Copilot, and Claude with web search may cite differently (Bing-fed engines favor Bing index; Google AIO favors Google index, where only 2 old CPC pages were indexed).
- Single run per prompt; no repeat runs to measure variance.
- I did not open the cited third-party pages to confirm listings or fees beyond what Perplexity returned in its snippets (e.g. Element Three, Boundify, Pisces details not verified).
- Review counts, Clutch pricing for the one fractional CMO firm, and directory inclusion criteria/costs were not verified.
- Did not verify which directories are free vs paid or whether CPC qualifies (Semrush agency directory requires partner status; Clutch needs client reviews).
- Did not check JSON-LD validity (Rich Results test) or on-page canonical on every page; only pattern-matched schema @types on 7 pages.
- Reddit/Quora/YouTube citation behavior not seen in these 8 prompts; may differ for other prompts and engines.
