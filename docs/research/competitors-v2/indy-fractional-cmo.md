## Testing CPC's claim: "most fractional CMOs hand you a plan without hands"

Verdict: largely true, but not universally, and it needs sourcing before it's a headline.

Evidence for:
- Lifted Logic, an agency that sells fractional CMO, writes that the CMO role is strategy, oversight and vendor management, and an agency is what "makes it happen". https://liftedlogic.com/fractional-cmo-vs-marketing-agency/
- Carissa Newton's offer is leadership, mentorship, vendor management, reporting. https://www.carissanewton.com/consulting
- JRCMO's plan is built to hand to an ad agency. https://jrcmo.com/agile/
- MarkCMO describes vendor and agency management as core. Madelyn Victoria's pricing breakdown puts strategy-only at $6,000-$15,000/mo, i.e. 2x-5x CPC.
- The marketingtoolshq piece says the "most common complaint" is a polished strategy in month one then no execution. Source is weak (vendor placement, anonymous interviews). Do not cite as a statistic.

Evidence against (use these to avoid overclaiming):
- Dala, Madelyn Victoria Co, TBH Creative, Marketing Guardians and Media Matters 317 all claim to execute. At least two are strategy plus hands at under $5,000 (Victoria) or at agency scale ($12K+ Guardians).
- So the true differentiator is not "we execute" alone. It is execute plus published price plus Indianapolis plus technical depth (web, GA4, GHL, AI, Ad Grants) at a retainer a small business can pay.

Suggested safe wording: "Many fractional CMOs are paid to direct, not to do. CPC does the work" rather than "most". Avoid a percentage.

1. Own the empty Indianapolis result: build a real "fractional CMO in Indianapolis" page with a street-level local angle (Indy founders, nonprofits, trades). Current page-one holders are a Canadian template page, a Florida programmatic page and a listicle with no local names.

4. Make the execution claim concrete: list deliverables per tier (posts, pages, ads managed) the way Madelyn Victoria does, and name the technical stack (GA4, GoHighLevel, Ad Grants, AI automation) that strategy-only CMOs and pure agencies skip.
5. Get listed: Clutch (Indianapolis fractional CMO), The Manifest, BrightLocal, CMO Index (free). Seek 3-5 reviewable client quotes first, since Clutch ranks reviews.
6. Use the 3-month minimum contrast: TBH requires one year; Madelyn Victoria matches 3 months. Mention "no year-long lock" only against TBH-style engagements.
7. Offer a low-friction entry like Newton's scorecard and JRCMO's free session: a free marketing/GA4 scorecard feeds the $3,000 strategy engagement.
8. Nonprofit angle is uncontested: none of the ten reviewed targets nonprofits or mentions Google Ad Grants. Lead with it.
9. Counter "best fractional CMO in Indianapolis" listicles: publish your own honest buyer's guide naming the checklist (hours per week, who owns ad accounts, execution included, minimum term).
