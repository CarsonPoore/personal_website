# Set A: Indianapolis marketing agencies (researched Oct 9, 2026)

Method: WebSearch for discovery, WebFetch (and firecrawl for Plaid) for page reads. Two or more pages per agency unless noted. "Not published" means not found on the pages read. Client-side result numbers are the agency's own claims.

## Seeds that did not pan out
- **Tomato Fish**: tomatofish.com redirects to a GoDaddy for-sale page. No live site; excluded.
- **Radius (Radius Media)**: only directory listings (BrightLocal, Gartner/UpCity) found; conflicting addresses (1706 Bellefontaine St vs 247 E 11th St). Directory lists a $2,000+/month minimum retainer and "enterprise" clients. Own site not verified; excluded from deep read. https://www.brightlocal.com/agency-directory/agencies/radius-media
- **Emulent**: surfaced for "Indianapolis small business" but is Raleigh/Wake Forest NC based; its Indianapolis page is a location-page SEO play. Not a real local competitor. https://emulent.com
- **Elevated Marketing Solutions**: elevatedmarketingsolutions.com is parked/for sale; real site is elevatedmarketing.solutions (read).

## What they ALL claim (CPC should not lead with it)
- "Full-service" and "integrated" marketing (8 of 10).
- Strategy first / data-driven / "real results" / ROI language, with no numbers on most home pages.
- "Custom, not cookie-cutter" and "no generic packages" (TBH, Ayokay, Elevated, EverEffect).
- Local roots and years of experience (16 to 48 years).
- Google Partner / awards badges.
- "Let's talk" / free consultation as the only next step.
- Anonymous "team of experts."

## What NONE of them do
- Publish the full price list across all services (only Moonflower and Firebelly show any numbers, and they cover one lane each).
- Name a retainer tier ladder with a stated minimum term and exit.
- Offer a fixed-fee starter website or logo with a starting price.
- Cap client count and say so (none states a cap).
- Put one named person as the person you work with, with a photo-led about (Moonflower is closest).
- Cover brand, web build, video/photo, Ad Grants and AI/automation under one roof for small businesses; most are PR/ads (Hirons, Plaid, Willow) or one lane (Firebelly social, Moonflower messaging).
- Mention Google Ad Grants for nonprofits as a service (none seen on pages read), or a reduced nonprofit rate.
- Publish location pages for central Indiana towns (EverEffect and Elevated have service/industry pages; no neighborhood or town pages seen in navs).
- Credit part of an entry engagement toward the retainer.

## Page-architecture notes
- Strong: EverEffect (service sub-pages, categorized blog), Elevated (industry pages, quiz, podcast), Hirons (9 services, 7 industries).
- Weak: Plaid and Willow (thin service copy), TrendyMinds (no services detail on home).
- Photography/design: most use stock-feeling or abstract imagery; Hirons and TrendyMinds look more polished; Plaid's home page copy is abstract. I did not visually render the sites, so design comments are inferred from page text only.
