# SERP teardown: local queries (researched Oct 9, 2026)

Method and limits. WebSearch returns an unordered, summarized set of about 9 links per query, not a literal ranked SERP, so "top results" below means the results the search tool surfaced, in the order given. No SERP features (People Also Ask, map pack, related searches) were visible to the tool, so PAA is marked "not visible" and the FAQ sections on competitor pages stand in as question-intent evidence. Word counts are estimates from WebFetch summaries. Title tags are often inferred from H1 when the head was not exposed. No keyword volume or difficulty tool was available (Ahrefs/Similarweb need auth), so difficulty is judgment from who ranks.

## Cross-query patterns

- Directory/listicle domains appear on nearly every query: Gartner agency directory, GoodFirms, Semrush Agency Partners, DesignRush, BrightLocal agency directory, UpCity/Clutch (cited on competitor pages), Krowdbase, Advids, Talent.com and Payscale (job/salary noise on "marketing consultant").
- Agency-published "Top N" lists are a major pattern, and the publisher ranks itself first. Web Tonic's Google Ads list (23 agencies) and social media list (23 agencies) are both published Sept 2026, updated Oct 9, 2026, with Web Tonic #1, 5 FAQ questions, a comparison table, and an "Indianapolis ... 2026 Disclosure List" angle. Source: https://www.webtonic.io/blog/best-google-ads-management-agencies-indianapolis and https://www.webtonic.io/blog/best-social-media-management-agencies-indianapolis. W3era does the same for SEO (https://www.w3era.com/blog/local-seo/top-10-seo-companies-in-indianapolis/, title "Top SEO Agencies in Indianapolis: 2026 Comparison Guide").
- Many "Indianapolis" service pages are from out-of-state shops: EZ Marketing (Lancaster, PA address), Knapsack Creative (Lynchburg, VA footer), Emulent (Wake Forest/Raleigh, NC), Thrive, Brandignity (Florida), Single Grain, Web Tonic. A genuinely local independent has a real proximity and entity advantage they cannot copy.
- Pricing is almost never published. Of every page read, only Knapsack Creative lists prices ($2,500 Fire-Starter and $4,950 Wildfire brand packages, https://knapsackcreative.com/branding-agency-for-indianapolis-in). Web Tonic's tables show $3,000/mo starting while saying "custom scope" and "none publishes a rate" (contradictory). Thrive shows budget-range form fields only ($2,500 to $50,000+/mo social). Everyone else: quote-based or not published. Published pricing is CPC's clearest differentiator.
- Freshness: most pages show no visible date, only a 2026 footer. The two Web Tonic lists carry explicit 2026 dates. Pages with "2026" in the title are the freshness norm.
- Title pattern: "[Service] Indianapolis | Brand" or "Indianapolis [Service] Agency | [tagline]"; listicles use "N Indianapolis [service] agencies 2026 | Brand". H1 usually repeats the query almost verbatim.
- FAQ depth: EZ Marketing 8 Qs, Market Veep 15 Qs, Knapsack 5, Web Tonic 5. Thrive, Brandignity, NUOPTIMA, Whole Whale, Nonprofit Megaphone: none.
