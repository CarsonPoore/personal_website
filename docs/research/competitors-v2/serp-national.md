# SERP teardown: national and question queries (serp-national)

Researched October 9, 2026. Method: WebSearch for each query (result order as returned by the tool, which approximates but is not a verified rank), WebFetch on the top pages and on CPC's own live pages. Word counts are estimates made by the fetch summarizer on article body only. Keyword difficulty (KD) is my judgment for a small independent site with a new domain, not tool data. No Ahrefs/Semrush data was available, and People Also Ask boxes are not exposed by WebSearch, so PAA is inferred from the FAQ sections of ranking pages.

CPC pages were fetched live at carsonpoore.com and confirmed to exist. Fetch tool strips scripts, so "no JSON-LD visible" means unverified, not absent. Check page source.

## 1. "what does a fractional cmo cost"

**Top results (domain, type):** thepricer.org (aggregator/blog), marketerhire.com (vendor blog), prosemedia.com (agency blog), chiefoutsiders.com (fractional firm blog), kalungi.com (B2B SaaS fractional firm blog), chatterbuzzmedia.com (agency blog), marketful.com (agency pillar page), kamyarshah.com (consultant blog), k6agency.com (agency blog).

**Title pattern:** "[How much does / What does] a Fractional CMO Cost[: ...] [2026]" with year, plus a subtitle like "pricing models, scope, and red flags" or "What to Budget." Near-universal year stamp.

**Depth of top 2:**
- marketerhire.com/blog/fractional-cmo-cost: ~2,500 words, $3,000-$15,000/mo answer in the first sentence, 5 tables (engagement types, models, fractional vs full-time, vs agency/freelancer, budget by stage), 5-question FAQ, byline Jenny Martin (Growth Marketing Editor), published April 15, 2026. Named stats: BLS median marketing manager comp ~$157,000 (2024); internal MarketerHire figures (30,000+ matches, 48-hour matching, 2-week trial). Source: https://marketerhire.com/blog/fractional-cmo-cost
- chiefoutsiders.com/blog/fractional-cmo-cost: ~1,100 words, answer NOT in first paragraph, no tables, no FAQ, byline Pete Hayes, created 2016, updated June 2024. Hourly bands ($100-$200 solo consultants up to $450+; exec-as-a-service retainers $1,500 to $30,000+/mo) and a worked ROI example on $10M revenue. Source: https://www.chiefoutsiders.com/blog/fractional-cmo-cost

**Related questions (inferred from FAQs):** monthly cost, vs agency, vs consultant, weekly hours, when to go full-time, retainer vs hourly vs project.

**Market consensus on price:** $3,000-$15,000/mo retainer (10-20 hrs/wk); $150-$350/hr advisory; project work $5,000-$25,000; some sources split $5,000-$12,000 for strategy-only and $15,000-$40,000+ with an execution team (search summary of thepricer, marketerhire, prosemedia, kalungi, chatterbuzzmedia). Entry freelance rates of $100-$150/hr reported in one source. All figures come from sellers.

**KD for small site:** High. Dominated by firms with domain authority, 2,000+ word pages, tables. The weakest ranker (Chief Outsiders, 2016, ~1,100 words, no table) shows a thin page can hold a spot, but only with brand authority.

**CPC page: /articles/fractional-cmo-cost** (~1,100 words, answer in paragraph one, one comparison table, 5 FAQs, byline Carson Poore, "Last updated: October 2026", cites MarketerHire/Chief Outsiders/Kalungi, publishes own $1,200/$2,400/$4,800).
- Winners have that CPC may lack: 2,000+ words; pricing tables by model (hourly/retainer/project) and by company stage or revenue tier; a cost-by-revenue table; full-time CMO cost comparison with an authoritative source (BLS); a visible date at publish and update; schema.
- **Single most valuable change:** add a "monthly budget by company revenue" table and a "retainer vs hourly vs project" table so CPC captures the same table-extractable answers, anchored by CPC's own sub-market price points ($1,200-$4,800) as the only published "small business" tier. That is a genuine gap: every winner starts at $3,000.

## Cross-query patterns

1. **Winners lean on tables and FAQs.** MarketerHire (5 tables, 5 FAQs), Mercury (stage/industry tables, 8 FAQs), GHL Scale Up (9 FAQs). CPC already has tables and 5 FAQs on most pages; add FAQPage schema (not verifiable as present).
2. **Freshness:** Most winners show 2026 dates (MarketerHire April 2026, Mercury July 2026, lowcode.agency September 2026, GHL Scale Up August 2026). CPC says "Last updated: October 2026" but shows no original publish date. Add datePublished/dateModified.
3. **Bylines:** Winners use editor/team bylines or exec names; CPC uses Carson Poore linked to About, which is stronger E-E-A-T than "Team" bylines.
4. **Data quality is weak across the field.** Many pages cite no sources or only their own metrics (Lifted Logic zero stats; Vendasta none; Chatmeter none). CPC's cited BrightLocal/SBA/vcita figures are an edge.
5. **Published prices are rare.** Only Pilot Digital (GA4), Valiant ($300/mo), and GHL vendor pages publish prices in the service queries. CPC's open pricing is a differentiator in queries 4, 8, and 9.
6. **Spam/low-quality slots:** the GoHighLevel queries include .edu/.ac.ke spam pages and sponsored syndications. Easiest openings.
7. **Page-type mismatch:** queries 4, 8, 9 are served by service pages, and 1-3, 5-7 by articles. CPC's mapping (articles for questions, service pages for consultant queries) is correct.
