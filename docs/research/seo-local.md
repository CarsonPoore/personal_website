# Research: seo-local

## Summary
SERP checks across ~11 queries show Indianapolis local-service keywords ("web design indianapolis", "google ads management indianapolis") are dominated by agency location/service pages plus directories (Clutch, BrightLocal, Semrush, UpCity) — winnable with a real local service page but crowded. "Marketing consultant indianapolis" is surprisingly soft: the SERP is mostly salary pages, job boards, and directories, with few strong individual-consultant service pages — this is Carson's best anchor keyword. "Fractional CMO indianapolis" is also thin (national roundups and off-topic CRO pages rank), making it a high-value low-competition target for the Coaching & Growth practice. Non-geo keywords ("gohighlevel expert", startup positioning) are national and rank blogs/marketplaces, so they belong to long-form content, not local pages. GBP + LocalBusiness JSON-LD (most-specific subtype, areaServed limited to real towns with supporting pages, exact NAP/URL consistency) is the connective tissue for all of it.

## Key findings
- "marketing consultant indianapolis" SERP is weak: top results are Payscale salary pages, Talent.com job listings, BBB/Gartner directories — almost no individual consultant service pages rank. Intent is mixed (job-seeker + hire-a-consultant), but the hire-intent slice is underserved. Page type that can win: a dedicated service/home page positioned as a person, not an agency. Competition: LOW-MEDIUM.
- "fractional cmo indianapolis" SERP is thin and low quality: a NUOPTIMA listicle, an India-based agency page, and multiple off-topic "fractional CRO" pages from pulserevops.com rank. No strong local practitioner page exists. Intent: hire/evaluate. Competition: LOW. This is the single best keyword-opportunity found — a dedicated Fractional CMO Indianapolis page should rank within months.
- "google ads management indianapolis" ranks programmatic location pages (webtonic.io, codewcg.com with suburb-level pages like "Near Greenwood"), agency blog posts styled as service pages (redefineweb.com), and a disclosure-listicle. Intent: hire, high commercial. Competition: MEDIUM — beatable by a genuine Indianapolis-based service page since most rankers are out-of-market SEO plays. One ranking page claims Indy DMA CPCs run below national average, but that's a vendor claim, not verified data.
- "web design indianapolis" is the most competitive of the set: established local firms (NextFly, Sharp Guys, Higgens Media), national agencies with city pages (Big Drop, Storm Brain), and roundup/directory content all rank. Intent: hire. Competition: HIGH. Target the softer variants: "web design for small business indianapolis", "website design carmel indiana", or lead with "websites & web apps" service page optimized for the broader term as secondary.
- "marketing agency carmel indiana" ranks directories first (BrightLocal agency directory, ClearlyRated, Clutch) plus strong incumbents headquartered there (Element Three, The Web Guys, TBH Creative, Ampfactor). Suburb-level agency terms are directory-dominated — better to target "marketing consultant carmel indiana" (consultant variant is softer) and name suburbs on service pages rather than build thin town pages immediately.
- "gohighlevel expert" is a NATIONAL keyword with marketplace/job-board SERP: Upwork jobs, Remotive listings, GoodFirms profiles, hire-guides (adsnipper.com "Hire a GoHighLevel Expert: Duties and Rates (2026)"). Intent splits hire vs. get-hired. Page type that ranks: long-form hire-guides and marketplace profiles. Play: a service page + a supporting guide ("What a GoHighLevel expert actually does / costs"); geo-modifier "gohighlevel consultant indianapolis" has essentially zero dedicated competition.
- "ai consultant indianapolis" SERP is programmatic and thin: Ademero city pages, aisuperior.com listicles, VarenyaZ blog-style pages, one Clutch profile. Only one genuinely local SMB-focused firm (Nahl Technologies) surfaced. Intent: hire/explore. Competition: LOW-MEDIUM. A page framed "AI agents & automation for small businesses — Indianapolis" fills a real gap; ranking competitors pitch enterprise ($5k/mo+ consulting), leaving the SMB angle open.
- "nonprofit marketing indianapolis" ranks one strong incumbent (RHB, a national nonprofit/higher-ed firm based in Indy) plus programmatic Krowdbase "Best SEO Companies for Nonprofits in Indianapolis 2026" listicles and Semrush directories. Intent: hire/research. Competition: LOW-MEDIUM. Ranking content references Google Ad Grants, donor lifecycle, recurring giving — the nonprofit page should speak that language. The reduced-rate offer is a differentiator no ranker leads with.
- "small business marketing indianapolis" ranks city-page SEO plays (emulent.com), Semrush directories, and institutional content (Indy Chamber, Colts small-business program). Intent: hire/research mixed. Competition: MEDIUM. Good secondary target for the home page and the local-business pathway page rather than its own page.
- Home-services vertical terms ("HVAC marketing indianapolis" family) are dominated by national niche agencies (iMarket Solutions 300+ contractors, Mediagistic, FieldEdge partner pages) — but none are Indianapolis-local. A trades/home-services pathway page can win on locality + the candid-friend voice against templated national players. The ranking pages model the intent language: customers search "AC repair in Indianapolis", "plumber near Indianapolis" — mirror that understanding on the page.
- Suburb searches ("marketing consultant fishers indiana") return almost nothing dedicated — AlphaGraphics Carmel blog posts, a Zionsville agency (Inspire Marketing) that lists served towns, Manifest directories. The winning pattern visible in SERPs: one strong page that NAMES the towns (Sharp Guys ranks for Carmel/Fishers/Noblesville queries by listing them) rather than thin per-town doorway pages.
- "business coach indianapolis" space is occupied by free/institutional options (Indy Chamber Business Ownership Initiative free coaching, ActionCOACH franchisees) — position Coaching & Growth as "fractional CMO / marketing advisor" not "business coach" to avoid both the franchise-coach association and the free-alternative anchor.
- Startup positioning keywords are national thought-leadership territory: SERP for positioning strategy is Substack essays, April Dunford/FletchPMM references, Maven course pages. No local page will rank; the founders pathway earns traffic via essays/guides and converts via the $3,000 strategy engagement page.
- GBP best practices 2026 (multiple sources): most-specific primary category; use all 750 characters of description leading with what + where, then differentiators (founder name, years in business, specializations); website URL must exactly match canonical format used in the sitemap; steady flow of RECENT reviews now beats total count, ideally mentioning services/keywords; respond to all reviews; claim and synchronize Yelp, Facebook, Bing Places, and Apple Business Connect with identical NAP.
- LocalBusiness schema 2026: JSON-LD; pick the most specific subtype (ProfessionalService fits a consultancy); minimum fields = name, url, telephone, address, openingHours, priceRange, geo, image; areaServed should list only 5-10 REAL towns as City objects (or GeoCircle for radius), each supported by on-page content; one clean NAP everywhere — schema/site/GBP mismatches now also degrade AI-engine citation, not just Google trust.
- Pair LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService with Service schema per practice page and FAQPage where the page carries real FAQs; sources frame schema explicitly as AI-readability infrastructure for 2026 (answer-engine citation), reinforcing plain-language on-page answers.
- Fractional CMO market pricing context (vendor-published, unverified precisely): typical engagements cited at $4,000-$10,000/mo part-time — Carson's $1,200-$4,800 retainers price below the category floor, which is worth stating plainly on the Coaching & Growth page (verify the comparison claim before publishing any specific competitor number).
- Directory presence matters for the suburb terms: BrightLocal, Clutch, ClearlyRated, GoodFirms, and Semrush Agency Partners pages occupy many page-one slots for "agency + town" queries — free/cheap profiles on these are page-one real estate Carson can't get with his own site for those exact queries.

- "fractional CMO" — searchers use this exact term; also "part-time CMO", "marketing leadership without a full-time hire"
- "Google Ads management" (not "PPC" first — PPC is the industry word, Ads is the owner word); "booked leads", "wasted spend", "cost per lead"
- "someone to run our Google Ads" / "is my agency wasting my money" — audit-intent framing seen across ranking pages
- "GoHighLevel expert" / "GHL" / "CRM setup" / "sales funnel" / "workflow automation" / "lead nurturing" / "pipeline" — the GHL hiring vocabulary (use sparingly given no-jargon voice; 'funnel' is banned in brand voice, so translate: "the path from first click to booked call")
- "AC repair in Indianapolis", "plumber near Indianapolis" — how trades customers search; shows the trades page should talk about ranking for THESE, not about 'local SEO'
- nonprofit vocabulary from ranking pages: "Google Ad Grants", "donor base", "recurring giving", "mission awareness", "board", "development director"
- founder vocabulary: "positioning", "who you serve and who you don't", "go-to-market", "messaging that converts" — April Dunford-shaped language
- "free business coaching" (Indy Chamber BOI) — the phrase the coaching page must NOT compete with
- geography strings people use: "central Indiana", "Indy", "the north side", "Hamilton County", town names Carmel / Fishers / Zionsville / Noblesville / Westfield / Greenwood
- "no long-term contract" / "what does it cost" — pricing-transparency queries; published pricing is rare among all rankers checked and is a standout signal
- "works with small businesses" vs rankers' "mid-market and enterprise clients" — the open positioning lane
