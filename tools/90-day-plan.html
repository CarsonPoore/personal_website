Your next 90 days on one page

Tell us your goal, who it’s for, what you sell, and the two things you’ll actually keep up with. You get a dated plan with a task for every week. Print it, copy it, tape it above your desk.

Five answers. One plan.

Best if it has a number and a date. You’ll check it on day 90.

The two channels you’ll keep up with

Pick exactly two. We think two channels, done every week, beat five done when you remember.

Everything stays in your browser. We never see what you type, and nothing is saved when you close the tab.

Thirteen weeks, five steps

The plan follows the same five steps we run every engagement through. They’re in the order we think they should happen, which is not the order most people try them.

  1. 01
    Conviction, week 1

    Why you built this and what you won’t bend on. Plus the goal, written so you can tell on day 90 if you hit it.

  2. 02
    Your people, week 2

    One real person, not a demographic. You talk to a few of them before you write another word.

  3. 03
    A sharp offer, weeks 3 and 4

    Your one sentence, cut until a stranger can repeat it back, then tested out loud on people who’d buy it.

  4. 04
    The experience, weeks 5 to 12

    Your two channels on a weekly rhythm that fits the hours you gave us. Eight weeks is long enough to see if a channel works.

  5. 05
    Word of mouth, week 13

    Review the numbers, ask your best customers for introductions, and decide the next 90 days. Read the five steps in full.

A plan from five boxes has blind spots

It doesn’t know your numbers. It can’t tell you if your offer holds up or if you picked the right two channels. It gives you a sound shape and a Monday to start. That’s worth having. It isn’t the whole job.

The whole job is the $3,000 strategy engagement: positioning, your people, the offer with the math behind it, and a 90-day plan we pressure-test with you. If you’re a founder deciding where to start, this page is for you. And if you’re not sure the engagement is worth it for your business, a free call will tell you in thirty minutes.
