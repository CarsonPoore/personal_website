Which chore should an agent take first?

Check the tasks you still do by hand. Tell us how many hours each one eats. You’ll get your best first automation named, with a plain verdict on every task: an agent, an automation, or honestly, just a better-connected CRM.

Start the finder → or see what we build

Free. No email. No login. Nothing to download. Everything you enter stays in your browser.

The math is short, and it’s all on this page.

Every task has a fixed ease rating from 1 to 5, set by us. A 5 means pure rule-following: copy this from there to here. A 1 means someone has to use judgment every time. The finder multiplies that rating by the hours you entered, and the biggest product goes first.

That’s the whole formula. It can’t know your margins or what your Tuesday nights are worth, so it doesn’t pretend to. The only hours it shows are the ones you typed in.

We think most five-person businesses should automate one task, watch it for a few weeks, and only then pick a second. That’s why the finder names one first pick instead of a shopping list.

An agent

Software that reads a message or a form, drafts a reply or a summary, and waits for your tap. It writes in your name, so it needs the most care to set up.

An automation

A fixed rule: when this happens, do that. No judgment involved, which makes it the cheapest kind of fix to reason about.

Just a better-connected CRM

The tools you own don’t talk to each other, or you don’t have a CRM yet. We’d connect things before building anything new.
