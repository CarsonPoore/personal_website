  2. Your peopleDo you know your best customers well enough to write to one of them by name?
  3. A sharp offerCan a stranger tell what you sell and why it’s worth it in about ten seconds?

Before you start

Do I have to give you my email to see my score?

No. There’s no email and no login. Your answers stay in your browser and nothing is saved. The only time your score leaves this page is if you click through to book a call, which carries the number along so we can prepare.

How is the check-up scored?

Each question has four answers worth 0, 3, 7, or 10 points. Two questions cover each of the five steps, so every step is scored out of 20 and the total is out of 100. Your weakest step is the one with the lowest score. If two tie, we name the earlier one, because the later steps stand on it.

What happens on the free call?

We spend 30 minutes on your weakest step. You’ll leave with one or two specific fixes, even if we never work together. We take eight clients at a time, so if we’re not the right fit, we’ll say so and point you somewhere better.
