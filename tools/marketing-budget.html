Where the percentages come from

The anchor is SBA guidance. The U.S. Small Business Administration recommends that businesses with under $5 million in annual revenue put 7–8% of gross revenue into marketing. Every result above is compared against it.

The stage bands are ours: steady is 5–7%, growing is 8–10%, new or turnaround is 10–12%. That’s a judgment call set around the SBA anchor, not a published standard.

What counts as marketing? All of it. Our fee if you hire us, ad spend, software, photography, printing, sponsorships, and the slice of someone’s salary spent on it. This part is boring. Do it anyway, because owners tend to compare a retainer against a budget that never included the other half.

One opinion: we think most small businesses should start at the low end of their range and move up only when the calls and booked jobs show it’s paying back.
