The standard

What a write-up has to carry.

We think a case study with no named client and no dated number is an ad. So ours won’t run without both.

  1. A named clientWith their permission. If they’d rather stay anonymous, the story isn’t ready.
  2. One number, with its dateCalls, booked jobs, or donations. Impressions don’t count.
  3. The starting problem, in the owner’s wordsNot ours, and not polished.
  4. What didn’t workEvery engagement has a month we’d redo. The write-up says which one.
